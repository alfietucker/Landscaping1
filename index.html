OUR STORY

Built From a Passion for Better Landscaping and Better Service

Elite Eagle Landscaping began in 2022 with a simple summer project and a lifelong passion for making outdoor spaces look their best. What started with lawn maintenance quickly grew into a full-service landscaping company serving homeowners, commercial properties, property managers, and communities throughout Perth and the greater Perth metro area.

Founder Damani Daniels has loved landscaping since childhood — planting, caring for grass, and paying attention to the small details that make a yard feel clean, healthy, and well-maintained. After seeing how other companies failed to put clients and employees first, he set out to build a business that does things differently: clear communication, quality work, reliable service, and genuine care from the first quote to the final walkthrough.

Today, Elite Eagle Landscaping continues to grow from its lawn care roots into landscaping installs, turf, irrigation, commercial maintenance, and hardscape services — always guided by the same commitment to detail, trust, and customer satisfaction.
